# Data, Claims, and Tool Transparency

Use this note when presenting the repository or discussing it in an interview.

- The examples and datasets in this repository are synthetic portfolio demonstrations.
- Do not describe them as paid client projects or production evaluation results.
- Do not report performance gains, model improvements, or reviewer-agreement statistics unless they have actually been measured.
- List software tools on a CV only if you have used them and can explain your workflow in an interview.
- Keep professional experience claims separate from the demonstration projects in this repository.
- The repository shows sample reasoning and a possible quality workflow; it does not by itself verify employment history or tool proficiency.
- The calibration cases in `projects/reviewer-calibration/` are self-review illustrations, not records of a paid quality-assurance cycle.
